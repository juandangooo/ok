# Edit plan: Hellhound Audio "Services We Offer" loop

Runtime 2:30.0 (source 2:29.6) · 1920x1080 (16:9, same ratio as the 1280x720 source) · 30 fps · silent by design · loops with no fade to black.

## What the source contained

- **Brand:** the HH hexagon mark (red / black), HELLHOUND AUDIO wordmark, hellhoundaudio.com, a QR code (it decodes to `HellhoundAudio.com`), a typed-URL line with a block cursor and a `:)`, and a VHS "PLAY" glitch intro.
- **Services (exact words):** Audio, Visual, Staging, Power, Truss, Lighting. In the source these words cycle every 2 s and are *not* matched to the photo beside them (a baby at a mixing console was labelled "Power"). This edit pairs each word with footage that shows it.
- **Footage:** about 73 still photos at 2 s each (no moving video apart from the intro). Slide *i* (i >= 1) is on screen at source time 4+2i to 6+2i s.

## Structure (a ring, not a list)

Six 24 s service segments, each with four 6 s photos, plus a 6 s brand moment, gives 150 s. Every frame is computed as a function of time modulo 150 s, so the end frame is a continuation of the first frame and the transition from last to first is rendered like any other.

| Escher idea | How it appears |
|---|---|
| Repetition | One layout on every segment: logo, title, six hexagon pips, QR, typed URL, photo window. Nothing moves except what changes. |
| Tessellation | A faint hexagon lattice sits behind everything and drifts exactly one cell over the 150 s loop, so it is seamless. |
| Gradual transformation | Photos change through a soft hexagonal iris (the logo's own shape), never a cut or flash. The hexagon pips and "What we offer" bullets are the same shape. |
| Matching relationships between scenes | Neighbouring photos rhyme: faders to faders, black curtain to black curtain (Audio to Visual), wide stage to wide stage, truss gateway to string lights. |

## Segment notes

- **Audio** (Sound systems and mixing): Hands on faders (the little one with headphones at the Behringer console) opens the loop; console, operator, and a speaker stack follow.
- **Visual** (Screens and video): Screens flanking a stage, the video switcher desk, an LED wall on turf, venue screens by the windows.
- **Staging** (Stages and platforms): A crew assembling a platform, a school stage, then two outdoor festival stages.
- **Power** (Power for your event): Crew beside road cases, rack cases, a rack of gear and cabling, a console on cases. See note 2.
- **Truss** (Truss structures): Hands assembling truss, truss over a gym stage, then two street truss gateways (Boro Bike Tour).
- **Lighting** (Stage and event lighting): String lights in a tent, blue wash on a small stage, moving heads at a wrestling event, a saturated stage that melts back into the logo.

## Type and colour

- Titles: DM Serif Display, up to 236 px (about 22% of frame height), dark brown-black on cream, roughly 13:1 contrast. Descriptors: DM Sans Medium 54 px. Nothing under 30 px.
- Palette: cream `#EEE4D2`, ink `#2A2420`, soft ink `#544840`, brick red `#B0302A` (from the logo), mustard `#E2A82B` (from the original's QR arcs, used only as a small rule).
- Photos: gently muted, a hint of warmth and lifted blacks, brightness evened out so no photo is a jump, light grain and vignette, shown as a print with a paper edge. Slow 5% push-in or pull-out, alternating.

## Things to confirm before this goes on the TVs

1. **Descriptors are my proposed copy.** The source only has one-word labels. "Sound systems and mixing", "Screens and video", "Stages and platforms", "Power for your event", "Truss structures", "Stage and event lighting" describe what the footage shows; edit them in `SERVICES` in `render_loop.py` and re-render, or tell me the wording you prefer.
2. **Power:** no photo in the source clearly shows power distribution. I used road cases, racks and cabling. If you have power-distro photos, swap them in.
3. **Resolution:** the photos only exist in the source video at 546 px square, and are shown at 760 px. If you can send the original photos, the whole loop gets noticeably crisper.
4. **People in photos:** a few shots show identifiable people (podium speakers, a baby, crew). They were already in your video, but confirm you are comfortable with them on a looping public display.
5. Tents, a Primark storefront and the two glitchy brand cards inside the source were left out: tents are not one of the six listed services, and the cards duplicate the bumper.

## Rebuild

```
python3 render_loop.py prep <source.mp4> <workdir>
python3 render_loop.py still <workdir> <seconds> <out.png>      # preview any moment
python3 render_loop.py chunk <workdir> <frameA> <frameB> <out.mp4>
```
(Fonts in `fonts/` are OFL: DM Serif Display, DM Sans, Cutive Mono. Needs ffmpeg, Pillow, numpy.)
